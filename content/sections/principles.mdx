When two good options conflict, the higher principle wins and the deprioritisation is written down. Principles are the compass; nothing here is automatically checkable, and that is the point.

They come in two scopes. Product design principles bind every domain. Brand principles belong to one domain each — Kind Utility is the Teachers & School declaration, and each new domain declares its own as part of [its profile](/domains).

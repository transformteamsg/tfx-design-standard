Teachers & School, Students, Parents, and Platform each get a page carrying
their brand profile — primary colours, typefaces, illustration direction,
voice, stack, and the products they cover. Teachers & School is settled and
launches rich; the other three are honest, structured stubs that double as
the onboarding template for their domain lead.

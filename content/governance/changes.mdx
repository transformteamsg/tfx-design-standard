Each entry records what shipped, in the order it landed. Proposals that have
not shipped live in [How this evolves](/governance), not here. The harness
plugin keeps its own detailed `CHANGELOG.md` in the repo; this page is the
readable summary.

## 13 July 2026 — Content controls fold into the division standard

- **Catalog 62 → 70.** The content-guidelines track lands: CNT-8
  (nominalisations), CNT-9 (clarity), CNT-10 (one term per thing), CNT-11
  (established terms), CNT-12 (sentence case), CNT-13 (spelling and Singapore
  English), CNT-14 (voice quality), and TYP-6 (comfortable measure) — seven
  content controls and one typographic one, all settled. See the
  [catalog](/standards/catalog).
- **Voice & tone.** A ⚑ proposed [voice & tone](/guidelines/voice-tone-proposed)
  guideline and refreshed UI-text, grammar, and web-interface pages arrive with
  them.
- **Conformance sweep.** Dogfooding the sprint's new diagrams against the now-70
  catalog: off-scale type sizes snapped onto the scale sitewide (TYP-3),
  `--muted-foreground` darkened so muted text clears WCAG AA on the `--muted`
  fill (A11Y-1), and the loop diagram's labels moved to real-px HTML so they
  stay legible on a narrow ring (TYP-2).

## 12 July 2026 — Motion foundation: tokens and two proposed controls

- **Catalog 57 → 60, recorded.** The June–July ratchet rounds added CNT-5,
  CNT-6, and CNT-7 as ⚑ proposed content controls. The additions predate this
  entry but were never recorded here; this closes the gap.
- **Catalog 60 → 62.** MOT-2 (motion values come from the declared token set,
  never hardcoded) and MOT-3 (motion may emphasise meaning but never carry it
  alone) join as ⚑ proposed, pending the design lead. Both are in the
  [catalog](/standards/catalog).
- **Motion tokens.** Four durations (`--motion-fast` to `--motion-story`) and
  two easings, declared in the site's token block and mirrored for motion/react
  by `lib/motion.ts`. The landing reveal and the diagram animations now consume
  them instead of hardcoding values.
- **Motion foundations page.** The duration scale, easing rules, when not to
  animate, and reduced motion — documented at [Motion](/foundations/motion),
  ⚑ proposed.

## 10 July 2026 — DXD program: one foundation, four domains

The standard's identity moved from TFX (a TransformX team standard) to DXD
(the division standard). One foundation now serves four domain expressions:
Teachers & School, Students, Parents, and Platform (including EduPass).

- **Domains section.** Four [domain pages](/domains), each a portfolio-level
  brand boundary with its own profile — colours, typography, voice. Teachers &
  School is settled; the other three are stubs awaiting their leads.
- **Domain registry and profile schema.** Profiles live in
  `standards/domains/<slug>.yaml`; catalog controls can scope to a domain.
- **De-branded skills.** Harness skills no longer hardcode Teacher & School
  branding — brand resolves from the domain profile at run time.
- **Domain onboarding.** Setup became a wizard that walks a new domain lead
  through declaring a profile, documented at [Get started](/harness/get-started).
- **Harness 0.8.0 rename.** Plugin id `tfx` → `dxd`, waivers written
  `dxd-waive` (legacy `tfx-waive` stays valid), context dir `.dxd/` with a
  `.tfx/` fallback. Control ids and catalog bodies are unchanged.
- **Governance roles.** Two named roles: the foundation owner approves
  ratchets; domain leads own their profile and domain-scoped controls.

## 8 July 2026 — Harness 0.7.0: ratchet and enforcement

- **Catalog 53 → 57 controls.** CMP-4 (empty states signal "no content
  exists"), CMP-8 (non-destructive exits, draft safety), CMP-9 (render-time
  sanitisation of cross-user content), CNT-4 (content modelling real-world
  artifacts is faithful or labelled illustrative).
- **Async-state evidence rule.** When loading/success/error states are in
  scope, the verify evidence must capture all of them — adopted as a harness
  rule in `design/verify.md`, deliberately not a new catalog category.
- **Enforcement as data.** New `enforced`/`script` fields in the catalog
  schema plus a coverage check, so how a control is checked lives in the
  catalog itself.
- **The grill.** The plan gate grew a red-team interview stage: the loop
  questions its own plan, one decision at a time, before asking for approval.

## 4 July 2026 — Harness 0.6.0: work by intent

- **Intent routing.** You say what you want to do; the harness picks the
  skill. Skill names no longer gate discovery.
- **Focused passes.** Five dimension-scoped passes — copy, polish, motion,
  flow, layout — improve one dimension of an existing page without the full
  loop.
- **Scoped rulebook.** Controls declare which products and audiences they
  bind, so a check run loads only the rules that apply.
- **Product identity in one place.** Each product carries a `DESIGN.md`;
  scattered brand assets consolidated there.

The full release note for 0.6.0, with before-and-after comparisons, is at
[harness-changes.html](/harness-changes.html).

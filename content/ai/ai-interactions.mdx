> Reference: [Google PAIR Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook/), [Microsoft HAX Toolkit](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/), [IBM Carbon for AI](https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/carbon-for-ai/), [Vercel AI Elements](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/). Rules below are distilled from the highest-agreement points across all four sources and adapted to TFX products and the Kind Utility brand.

## Start with what you're designing

Choose your surface from the task type first.
The wrong surface creates busywork — a teacher who already knows what they want should never have to compose a prompt to get it.

| What you're designing | Where it lives | Components to reach for | The call that matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inline suggestion — autocomplete, chip, next action | Embedded in the existing workflow; no separate panel | `Suggestion`, `PromptInput` | Does the suggestion interrupt the task or fit inside it? |
| Generated draft — write or rewrite a field or section | Inline in the form or document; modal only for full-page replacement | `Message`, `MessageResponse`, `Shimmer` | Is the output editable, revertable, and clearly marked? |
| Summarisation — distil a document or thread | Inline below or beside the source | `Message`, `Sources`, `InlineCitation` | Can the teacher trace every claim back to its source? |
| Q&A over content — ask about a document or thread | Embedded panel or drawer beside the source | `Message`, `Sources`, `InlineCitation`, `Attachments` | Does it ask rather than guess when it is not confident? |
| Agent that takes actions — files, sends, creates records | Inline workflow with an explicit confirmation gate; never silent | `Confirmation`, `Task`, `Plan`, `Checkpoint` | Is every consequential action shown in plain language before it runs? |
| Classification output — label, flag, or score on a record | Inline with the field it annotates; never silent | `Message`, `Sources`, `InlineCitation` | Is the teacher meant to act on it or just know it? If act, add a confirmation path. |
| Transcription — voice dictation into a field | Inline in the form; transcribed text immediately editable | `SpeechInput`, `Transcription` | Faithful transcription of the teacher's words, or reinterpretation? If reinterpreting, route to generated draft. |
| Full conversation — open-ended chat | Last resort, not first idea — see [Conversation UX](/ai/conversation-ux) | `Conversation`, `Message`, `PromptInput`, `Reasoning` | Is this task genuinely open-ended, or does it have a known shape? |

- **Use an embedded, task-specific control when the job is known. Use a conversation surface only when the task cannot be scoped in advance.** An assistant that takes actions routes to the agent row, even when it is framed as chat. Before routing, ask whether AI belongs at all - if a rule, filter, or manual control gives the same result with less uncertainty, prefer it. The Q&A row covers a known, bounded document set; when the source set is open-ended, the conversation row applies.

  _([PAIR "User Needs + Defining Success" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/user-needs/) - "do not use AI just because you can")_

## Rules that always apply

### Be honest about the AI

- **Set expectations before the first interaction.** The teacher should know what the feature can and cannot do before they rely on it.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | "This summary draws only from submitted reading records. It can't access verbal assessments." | Show a CaseSync summary with no indication of what data it used or what it might miss. |

  _([HAX G1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/make-clear-what-the-system-can-do/)-[G2](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/make-clear-how-well-the-system-can-do-what-it-can-do/); [PAIR "Mental Models" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/mental-models/))_

  <DemoEmptyState />

- **Mark AI-generated content at the point where it appears.** The label stays until the teacher edits the field; it sits at the smallest scope that covers the AI content.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | Show an AI label on the "End-of-term comment" field when the assistant drafted it; remove it once the teacher edits the text. | Put the AI notice only in the page header and leave individual fields unmarked. |

  _([Carbon AI label principle](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/ai-label/usage/) - placement scoping; [HAX G11](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/make-clear-why-the-system-did-what-it-did/))_

  <DemoAiLabel />

- **Show certainty in plain language, not percentages.** Label output reliability as high, medium, or low — or offer the top alternatives.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | "Low confidence — the band level was last updated 14 weeks ago. Check the record before using this." | "63% confidence" next to a suggested reading band for a student in Class 5A. |

  _([PAIR Pattern 11 - "Determine how to show model confidence, if at all"](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/patterns/); [HAX G2](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/make-clear-how-well-the-system-can-do-what-it-can-do/))_

  <DemoConfidence />

### Keep the teacher in charge

- **The teacher stays the author.** AI output is always editable and revertable; editing removes the AI mark, reverting restores it.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | Let the teacher reword a drafted end-of-term comment directly in the field; remove the AI label when they save their edit. | Lock the generated comment to read-only with a "regenerate" button as the only path forward. |

  _([Carbon revert-to-AI pattern](https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/carbon-for-ai/); [HAX G9](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/support-efficient-correction/), [G15](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/encourage-granular-feedback/); [PAIR "Feedback + Control" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/feedback-controls/))_

- **Consequential actions need explicit approval; everything else gets undo.** Any AI-triggered action that modifies, sends, or deletes records shows the consequence before it runs.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | "This will update Amir's reading level in CaseSync from Band 4 to Band 6. Confirm?" with Approve and Deny. | Update the reading record silently and show a toast: "Record updated." |

  _([HAX G8](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/support-efficient-dismissal/), [G9](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/support-efficient-correction/); [PAIR Pattern 16 - "Let users supervise automation"](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/patterns/); Vercel AI Elements [`Confirmation`](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/confirmation) component)_

  <DemoConfirmation />

- **Acknowledge feedback at the point it is given.** Confirm receipt in the same view; never promise the model will improve.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | Show "Feedback received" inline below the flagged suggestion immediately after the teacher thumbs it down. | Dismiss the flag silently, with no visible acknowledgement. |

  _([PAIR "Feedback + Control" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/feedback-controls/); [HAX G15](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/encourage-granular-feedback/))_

  <DemoFeedback />

### Handle doubt and failure safely

- **Scope when uncertain; ask before acting on a wrong assumption.** When the AI is not confident about intent or the right action, it narrows its scope or asks — it does not proceed on a guess.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | "I can see two students named Sarah in Class 5A. Which record did you mean?" before drafting any case note. | Pick the first match and draft a case note for the wrong student. |

  _([HAX G10](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/scope-services-when-in-doubt/); [PAIR "Errors + Graceful Failure" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/errors-failing/) - context errors)_

  <DemoClarify />

- **Make failure safe and boring.** Error messages say what did not work and what the teacher can do next — no ML or model terminology.

  | Do | Don't |
  | --- | --- |
  | "Couldn't generate a summary right now. You can write one manually or try again in a moment." | "Model inference failed. Token limit exceeded." |

  _([PAIR "Errors + Graceful Failure" chapter](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/errors-failing/) - "make failure safe and boring"; [TFX CNT-1](/standards/catalog/cnt-1))_

## Product calibration

Same rules everywhere; weight and tone adjust by product.

| Product | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher Workspace | Standard | Inline suggestion and generated draft are the most likely first features. Error tone: workmanlike — no apology, no cheerfulness, just the next step. |
| CaseSync | Higher gravity | Records feed assessments and may affect student placement. The authorship rule and the approval rule carry extra weight. Confirmation copy surfaces the student name, assessed item, and proposed value — not just "Confirm". |
| Glow | Lighter | Inline suggestion fits goal-setting and reflection surfaces. Failure tone can be warmer. The AI marking must still be present — no lighter treatment waives the labelling requirement. |

---
title: Conversation design
description: Conversation surfaces work best when no structured control can do the job. Know when to reach for chat - and how to make it trustworthy when you do.
status: proposed
---

- **Conversation is the last resort, not the first idea.** Embedded AI inside the task beats chat one step removed from it. Reach for a conversation surface only when the teacher's goal cannot be known in advance. See [AI design](/guidelines/ai-design) for the routing table that helps you decide.

- **Prompt input carries the guidance.** Suggestions show what the assistant is good at. Attachment indicators show what it can read. A teacher who can see both knows immediately how to begin.

  <DemoPromptInput />

- **Streaming must be stoppable.** Progress is visible while the response generates, and the teacher can stop it at any point without losing what arrived so far. No generation leaves the teacher locked out mid-turn.

  <DemoStreaming />

- **Grounded answers show their sources.** Every factual claim traces back to a record the teacher can open. A response without sources is a response a teacher cannot verify or act on with confidence.

  <DemoSources />

- **Consequential actions stop for approval.** Before the assistant files, sends, or modifies a record, it shows the consequences in plain language and waits for an explicit yes. This is the conversational form of CMP-2.

  <DemoConfirmation />

- **Refusals and errors follow the same anatomy as any TFX error (CNT-1): what happened, what it means, what to do next.** No model talk. No apology language. Failure stays safe and boring.

- **Scope when in doubt.** When the teacher's intent is ambiguous, the assistant asks one clarifying question rather than acting on a guess. Acting on the wrong assumption costs more than a single turn of delay.

- **Conversation memory is visible.** The thread shows what the assistant can see in this session. Any context the teacher attached - a document, a student's notes, a class list - stays visible so they know exactly what the assistant is working from.

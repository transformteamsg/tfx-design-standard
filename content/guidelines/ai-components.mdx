> Reference: [Google PAIR Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook/), [The Shape of AI](https://www.shapeof.ai/), [AIverse](https://www.aiverse.design/), [Vercel AI Elements](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/), [MIT Human-AI Impact Bench](https://impactbench.media.mit.edu/).

[Use cases](/guidelines/ai-patterns) covers which kind of AI feature to build and how it should behave.
This page is the parts you build one from, laid out along the five stages a person moves through.
In each stage table, "reach for it when" and "don't use it when" are the whole decision.

Most parts are AI Elements components, a library you copy into your codebase and then own and edit.
A few are patterns with no component of their own.
They are built from shadcn primitives (plain parts like Badge and Alert), or from a slot on an installed component.
Your coding agent can install and wire all of it; this page is the judgement.

## Shapes AI takes in a product

An AI feature shows up in a product in more than one shape.
The same parts below serve every shape; the shape you build decides which parts do the most work.

- **Chat** - a back-and-forth thread. Most demos on this page use it because the parts show up all at once. → [Conversations](/guidelines/ai-patterns#conversations)
- **Inline** - the AI lives inside another surface: autocomplete in a field, a "suggest" button that fills a form, a next-step chip beside your work. → [Suggestions and next steps](/guidelines/ai-patterns#suggestions-and-next-steps)
- **Agent or workflow** - a multi-step task the AI runs on someone's behalf, with a plan and gates, sometimes with no chat surface at all. → [Agents that take action](/guidelines/ai-patterns#agents-that-take-action)
- **Classifier** - the AI adds a label, score, or flag to a record. The interface is usually a table row, not a conversation. → [Classifying and flagging](/guidelines/ai-patterns#classifying-and-flagging)
- **Summariser panel** - the AI condenses source material, shown beside or above the source. → [Summarising and transforming content](/guidelines/ai-patterns#summarising-and-transforming-content)
- **Voice** - hands-free, with a record button and a transcript view. Fits some accessibility and dictation cases.

The stages below apply across all of them.
An inline suggestion has a Discover and an Oversight; a classifier has an Output and an Oversight; an agent has a Progress and an Oversight.
The shape decides how each stage is expressed.

## Discover

Give a person their first move, before they have asked for anything.

| Component | What it is | Reach for it when | Don't use it when | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Empty state | The opening panel of a chat, before anyone has typed. | The surface is open-ended and a first-time user would not know what to ask. | The surface does one job and could just do it. "Ask me anything" adds a step to something that should already run. | `ConversationEmptyState` slot, plus `Suggestions` |
| [Suggestion](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/suggestion) | Tappable chips offering a prompt or a next step. In a chat, they open the exchange; inline, they fill a form field or nudge the next move. | You can name two or three moves genuinely useful to this person now. | You cannot. Generic chips like "Tell me more" train people to skip the row. | `Suggestions` around `Suggestion` chips |

## Instruct

How a person tells the AI what they want, and what it runs on.

| Component | What it is | Reach for it when | Don't use it when | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Prompt input](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/prompt-input) | A text box with tools, attachments, and a send control that doubles as stop. | The request is genuinely open and you cannot list the options in advance. | The task has a known shape. A form with three fields beats a blank box someone must guess at. | `PromptInputBody` and `PromptInputFooter` |
| [Attachments](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/attachments) | A strip of files the person has added to the request. | The answer depends on a document only that person holds, such as a parent uploading a report. | The record already lives in your product. Reference it rather than asking for a re-upload. | `Attachments` with `Attachment` and `AttachmentRemove` |
| Clarify | One question back from the AI before it acts. | The request has two plausible readings and guessing wrong would waste the person's work. | You can infer the answer from context you hold. A chain of clarifiers is a form in disguise, so build the form. | `Message` plus `Suggestions`, one chip per reading |

A few more parts belong in this stage only when the situation calls for them.
**Speech input** and **Transcription** turn talk into text, but not where saying a learner's name aloud is a disclosure.
**Mic selector** belongs after a failed recording, not as a first-run setting.
**Context**, **Model selector** and **Voice selector** expose the model itself.
They earn their place only when the person genuinely manages cost, speed, or accent.
Naming a model to someone with no basis to choose makes the outcome their fault.
**Persona** is an animated character with listening and thinking states, for a voice surface.
A face in a learning or assessment setting invites trust the system has not earned.

## Progress

Tell a person the AI is working, which step it is on, and what it has done.

| Component | What it is | Reach for it when | Don't use it when | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Shimmer](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/shimmer) | A moving highlight over text while it streams, meaning arrives progressively. | Text is actively arriving and you want the eye to follow the leading edge. | Nothing is streaming. A shimmer with no text arriving reads as a stall. | `Shimmer` |
| [Task](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/task) | One step of work, with its own state and any files it touched. | Work runs in visible steps and someone may need to see which one failed. | There is only one step. A single row with a tick is ceremony. | `Task` with `TaskTrigger` and `TaskContent` |
| [Plan](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/plan) | The ordered steps an agent intends to run, before it runs them. Works inside a chat, and equally well on a standalone approval queue for a workflow. | The steps are genuinely under review before anything happens. | They cannot be edited or refused. A plan nobody can change is a progress bar in costume. | `Plan` with `PlanContent`, `PlanAction` and `PlanFooter` |
| [Tool](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/tool) | A collapsible record of one tool call, where a tool reaches outside the model. | The call touched a real record or the outside world, so it doubles as evidence. | It is an internal call with no consequence. A wall of expanded tool calls is noise. | `ToolHeader` with its `state` prop, plus `ToolInput` and `ToolOutput` |

Also here: **Loader** for a wait of a second or two.
**Queue** holds stacked requests, though a visible backlog often advertises a wait you have not fixed.

## Output

Show the result, mark it as AI, and let a person check where it came from.

| Component | What it is | Reach for it when | Don't use it when | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Message](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/message) | One turn from a person or the AI, with its own toolbar. | The exchange is genuinely back and forth. | It is a one-shot draft inside a form. A chat bubble implies a conversation that is not there. | `Message` with `MessageContent` and `MessageResponse` |
| [Sources](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/sources) | A collapsible list of what an answer was grounded in, meaning drawn from named material. Sits under a chat reply, or beside a summary panel next to the source document. | The answer came from a bounded set the person can open and check. | The list was assembled afterwards. Sources that did not produce the answer borrow credibility they have not earned. | `Sources` with `SourcesTrigger`, then one `Source` per document |
| [Inline citation](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/inline-citation) | A citation marker sitting inside the sentence it supports. | Different sentences come from different places and the person must tell which is which. | One continuous claim comes from one source. A marker per sentence makes the text unreadable. | `InlineCitation` with `InlineCitationSource` |
| Confidence | A plain-language band on a result: high, medium, or low. Reads beside a chat reply, but earns its keep on a classifier row where low confidence routes to a person for review. | Reliability genuinely varies and low confidence unlocks a different action, such as routing to a person. | There is no different action. A badge you cannot act on shifts blame to the reader. | `Badge` in a fixed set of bands, beside the `Message` or the flagged record |
| AI label | A badge marking AI-produced content, with the explanation behind it. Sits on a chat reply, a drafted field, a table row's AI-set column, or any surface where a person might mistake AI output for their own. | Any AI output a person could mistake for their own work or a colleague's. | They have edited it. Once someone rewrites the text it is theirs, and a stale label misattributes their work. | `Badge` as a `Popover` trigger, plus a revert `Button` |

A few more parts sit in this stage.
**Conversation** holds the scrolling thread.
**Code block** is for output someone will copy, and **Image** only where accuracy does not matter, since generated images invent text and faces.
**Reasoning** and **Chain of thought** show the model's thinking, but that is narration, not a log.
It cannot justify a decision about a person.
**Open in chat** hands the query to an outside product, sending that text to a third party outside your data agreements.

## Oversight

Approve before it acts, then correct what is wrong.

| Component | What it is | Reach for it when | Don't use it when | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Confirmation](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/confirmation) | An approve-or-deny gate naming exactly what will happen. Wraps an agent action in chat, or gates a workflow step on a standalone approval queue. | Something sends, files, or changes a record about a person. | The action is small and reversible. Gating everything trains people to click through the one that matters. | `Confirmation` with `ConfirmationActions` and `ConfirmationAction` |
| [Checkpoint](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev/components/checkpoint) | A named point in a long run that someone can return to. | The run is long or branching, and a wrong turn would cost real work. | The flow is short, or you cannot actually restore the state you offer. | `Checkpoint` with `CheckpointTrigger` |
| Feedback | Thumbs up and down on a response, with an acknowledgement. Sits on a chat reply's toolbar, or on a classifier row so the person the label describes can correct it. | The loop visibly closes: the rating routes somewhere and the person sees it landed. | Nothing changes. A rating that goes nowhere collects free labour and teaches people to stop giving it. | `MessageToolbar` with `MessageAction`, plus a notice on the negative path |
| Error | A failure notice saying what happened and what to do next. | The AI failed and there is a real next move, even if that move is to do it by hand. | Retrying cannot work. Do not offer "Try again" for a refusal or a missing permission. | `Alert` with a `Button` and `Spinner` for the retry |

Error copy names the problem in the person's own terms.
Never surface model or infrastructure wording such as rate limits or context length.

## One stage this standard does not ship yet

The sixth thing AI does is remember across sessions, carrying what it learnt about a person from one visit to the next.
Nothing here ships for it.
Checkpoint restores a point inside one run and Context reports the current conversation, but both end when the session does.
If you need memory, treat it as new work: design what gets stored, show the person that record, and let them edit and delete it.

For the patterns this standard deliberately does not ship, and how to recognise and cost them, see the `ai` skill's reference notes.

The rules here are distilled from these and adapted to TFX products.

This page is for any designer adding AI to a product.
Hold onto three principles, then jump to the use case that fits what you are building.

## Three principles that always apply

Good AI does less work for the teacher, not more.
These three principles hold everywhere.

**Fit it to the task.**
Use the lightest AI that fits, and often that is none.
A suggestion beats a chatbot when you already know what the teacher wants.

**Make it honest.**
Mark what the AI wrote, show where it came from, and say how sure it is.

**Keep the teacher in control.**
The teacher edits, approves, and can undo.
Nothing that sends, files, or changes a record happens without a clear yes.

## What are you building?

Find your situation, then open [AI patterns](/guidelines/ai-patterns) for how to build it.

| You are building | The pattern |
| --- | --- |
| A hint or next step, inline | [Suggestions and next steps](/guidelines/ai-patterns#suggestions-and-next-steps) |
| A first draft of a field or note | [Generating content](/guidelines/ai-patterns#generating-content) |
| A short version of a long thing | [Summarising and transforming content](/guidelines/ai-patterns#summarising-and-transforming-content) |
| An answer from records or documents | [Answering questions from your data](/guidelines/ai-patterns#answering-questions-from-your-data) |
| A label, score, or flag on a record | [Classifying and flagging](/guidelines/ai-patterns#classifying-and-flagging) |
| An action taken on the teacher's behalf | [Agents that take action](/guidelines/ai-patterns#agents-that-take-action) |
| An open-ended chat | [Conversations](/guidelines/ai-patterns#conversations) |

## How these pages fit together

- **Overview** - the three principles, and which use case fits your situation.
- **[AI patterns](/guidelines/ai-patterns)** - what to build and how it should behave.
- **[Using components](/guidelines/ai-components)** - the AI Elements pieces you build it with.
- **[Writing prompts](/guidelines/ai-prompts)** - how to write the assistant's instructions.

AI patterns is the behaviour; Using components is the pieces.
Start with the pattern, then pick the components it needs.

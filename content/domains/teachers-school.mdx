Teachers & School is the TransformX portfolio for Singapore's teachers: Teacher
Workspace, CaseSync, and Glow. All three read as one family, calibrated by
product rather than reinvented per product.

## Brand profile

- **Colour:** Teacher Workspace anchors the portfolio in TW Blue
  (`--tw-blue` `#0064FF`). Glow uses Radix `orange-9` (`--glow` `#F76B15`,
  confirmed) — pair it with a dark foreground, not white. CaseSync's primary,
  Radix `indigo-9` (`--casesync` `#3E63DD`), is still proposed and awaits a
  design-lead settle (see [COL-1](/standards/catalog/col-1)).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans (600) for display, Inter (400/500/600)
  for body (see [TYP-1](/standards/catalog/typ-1)). Glow's wordmark is the one
  exception — its logo lockup sets in Inria Sans; every other typographic use
  stays on the shared pair.
- **Stack:** Base UI components, Radix Colors, and shadcn/ui default tokens.
- **Illustration:** direction not yet declared — foundation defaults apply
  until the domain lead adds one.
- **Voice:** Kind Utility — useful first, kind at the surface. The full
  declaration is this domain's [brand principles](/principles/brand-principles).

## Products

- [Teacher Workspace](/products/teacher-workspace) — the calm daily command
  centre, and the reference build every other product measures against.
- [CaseSync](/products/casesync) — sensitive student casework, so the design
  carries more gravity.
- [Glow](/products/glow) — the encouragement layer, lighter and warmer.

## Domain-scoped controls

- [IDN-4](/standards/catalog/idn-4) applies to CaseSync only: casework is
  treated as sensitive, so celebration stays restrained and nothing around
  case data is gamified.
